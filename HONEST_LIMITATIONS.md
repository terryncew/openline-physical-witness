# Honest Technical Limitations & Declared Holds (`HONEST_LIMITATIONS.md`)

1. **Zero Physical Fabrication or Wet-Lab Measurement**: All results in this repository are computational. No silicon/fused-silica wafers have been fabricated, and no wet-loop crossflow data have been collected.
2. **Conventional Multi-Sensor Fusion Wins When Soluble CP Is Absent (`S1`)**: When bulk conductivity is stationary and soluble concentration polarization is absent ($\text{CP} = 1.0\times$), calibrated multi-sensor fusion (`B3`: `0.67%` mean error) and calibrated single-arm $3\omega$ + flow + EC (`B2`: `0.91%` mean error) outperform `OTDB-APW Rev D` (`3.96%` mean error).
3. **Unflagged Partial Reference-Arm Fouling Vulnerability (`NC5`)**: While the firmware witness detects complete reference-cavity occlusion (`v3w_diff_re < 12 uV` when `v3w_sense_re > 440 uV`), **partial reference-arm fouling ($35\%\text{--}75\%$ of sense-arm thickness)** is not caught by the current threshold and causes `OTDB-APW Rev D` to underestimate scale thickness with **`71.17%` mean error**, whereas `B3` Multi-Sensor Fusion achieves **`7.72%` mean error**. Adding an independent optical or quartz-crystal reference check, or tightening the differential IDE admittance drift gate (`dy_diff_im_us`), is an open research problem for Phase-1 hardware.
4. **Operating Window Boundaries (`NC1..NC4`)**:
   * **Macro-Cake Ceiling (`>85 um`, `NC1`)**: Thermal penetration depth at $f_{1\omega} = 4.0\text{ Hz}$ is $L_p \approx 84.3\,\mu\text{m}$; above $85\,\mu\text{m}$, `OTDB-APW` saturates and must abstain in favor of differential pressure ([`Endress+Hauser PMD75B`](https://www.transcat.ca/endress-hauser-differential-pressure-deltabar-pmd75), `1.14%` error).
   * **Low-Conductivity Floor (`<0.5 mS/m`, `NC2`)**: In $18.2\text{ M}\Omega\cdot\text{cm}$ ultrapure water, electrolyte impedance exceeds the $10\text{ M}\Omega$ lock-in range; coaxial conductivity sensors ([`Thornton 240-202`](https://www.servapure.com/Thornton-Sensors_c_5965.html?page=2), `0.85%` error) win.
   * **Sub-150 nm Conditioning Films (`NC3`)**: Below the $0.48\,\mu\text{m}$ $3\sigma$ sensitivity floor, acoustic microbalances ([`Biolin QSense Pro` QCM-D](https://www.nanoscience.com/products/qsense-quartz-crystal-microbalance/qsense-pro/), `3.40%` error) win.
   * **Non-Stationary Multiphase Emulsions (`NC4`)**: Coalescing oil droplets violating the $1.2\text{ s}$ lock-in stationarity assumption force abstention (`ABSTAIN_NON_STATIONARY_INTERFACE`).
5. **Declared EDA & Cleanroom Holds (`HOLD_NATIVE_KICAD_CLI_ABSENT` & `FOUNDRY_HOLD_1..6`)**: Native `kicad-cli` ERC/netlist export and cleanroom foundry PDK DRC signoff must be executed on an external engineering workstation before ordering PCBs or photomasks.
