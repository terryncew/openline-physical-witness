# Plain-Language Scientific Abstract (158 Words)

Solid mineral scale and particulate fouling degrade reverse-osmosis membranes, heat exchangers, and electrochemical cells, yet conventional inline electrical impedance spectroscopy (EIS) and single-arm thermal sensors suffer from a fundamental identifiability bottleneck: soluble concentration-polarization (CP) salt layers perturb interfacial impedance without solid precipitation, while flow and viscosity fluctuations perturb single-arm convective heat transfer. We present the computational design, connected electronic schematic, C99 lock-in firmware, and synthetic falsification suite for the **Orthogonal Thermo-Electro-Differential Bridge and Fisher-Rank Active Physical Witness (OTDB-APW Rev D)**. The architecture pairs a fouled sense arm and hydrodynamically stepped reference arm on a single $3.5\times 3.5\text{ mm}$ fused-silica die passivated with a $30\text{ nm }\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3 / 30\text{ nm }\text{HfO}_2$ bilayer. Across literature-inspired synthetic crossflow scenarios, calibrated multi-sensor fusion outperforms OTDB-APW under constant-conductivity fouling (`0.67%` vs `3.96%` error) and partial reference-arm fouling (`7.72%` vs `71.17%`), whereas OTDB-APW reduces estimation error under unmeasured $1.35\times\text{--}2.15\times$ soluble CP (`8.17%` vs `43.31%`). Zero physical wafers have been fabricated; we publish a blind wet-loop falsification protocol.
